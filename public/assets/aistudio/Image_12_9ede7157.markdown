## Brand & Style
This design system occupies the intersection of clinical diagnostic rigor, high-fashion Parisian couture, and predictive intelligence. Designed for affluent, aesthetic-conscious users seeking bespoke cosmetic and wardrobe curation, the interface projects authoritative elegance, discretion, and bespoke craftsmanship.

The visual direction merges **Dark Glassmorphism** with **Editorial Precision**:
- **Atmospheric Depths:** Dark obsidian and slate foundations simulate private studio mirrors and high-end photographic suites.
- **Optoelectronic Precision:** Prismatic violet-indigo and glowing amber accents indicate active algorithmic analysis, biometric scanning, and tailored recommendations.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast editorial typography juxtaposed with architectural data layouts evokes the gravitas of bespoke atelier catalogs and high-precision dermatological reports.
- **Tactile Refinement:** Subtle optical boundary lines (inner borders), specular frosted glass sheens, and controlled bioluminescent glows eliminate visual clutter while enhancing spatial dimensionality.

## Colors
The palette balances obsidian depths with photonic accents to evoke biometric scanning against a velvet studio backdrop:

- **Primary Violet-Indigo (`#8B5CF6`, `#6366F1`):** Represents algorithmic intelligence, vision parsing, and high-frequency scan indicators. Used for primary CTAs, active tracking nodes, and system focus rings.
- **Secondary Rose-Amber (`#FB7185`, `#F59E0B`):** Conveys human warmth, bespoke luxury, skin undertone fidelity, and VIP styling tiers. Used for curation highlights, seasonal color matching, and couture badges.
- **Tertiary Clinical Emerald (`#10B981`):** Reserved for diagnostic readouts: epidermal hydration scores, barrier integrity, pigment stability, and validation states.
- **Surfaces & Neutral Base:**
  - Base Foundation: `#0B0F17` (Deep Obsidian).
  - Raised Surface: `#121826` (Slate Night).
  - Glass Tier: `rgba(18, 24, 38, 0.72)` with hairline specular highlights.
- **Text & Contrast:**
  - High Emphasis: `#F8FAFC` (Pure Alabaster) for maximum legibility.
  - Medium/Muted: `#94A3B8` (Cool Platinum) for technical readouts and auxiliary metadata.
  - Border Accents: `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.08)` to `rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`.

## Typography
The typographical hierarchy creates dynamic tension between high-fashion couture and scientific measurement:

- **Display & Headlines (`Bodoni Moda`):** Delivers the aristocratic editorial atmosphere of Vogue or Harper's Bazaar. Headlines utilize italic variants selectively for focal terms (e.g., *Sartorial Archetype*, *Dermal Metrics*).
- **Body & Technical Readouts (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides pristine legibility under complex lighting and camera feeds. Its modern geometric curves maintain humanist warmth without compromising clinical precision.
- **Labels & Micro-indicators:** Rendered in uppercase with generous letter spacing (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) to replicate instrument dials, clinical telemetry, and bespoke tailor classification labels.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a responsive fluid-grid framework constructed on an 8pt architectural rhythm, maintaining dramatic open space around core visual assets:

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Focuses on vertical camera feeds, floating bottom sheets, and full-bleed viewport preview zones.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Introduces split-view diagnostic telemetry alongside dynamic render models.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns with a max content envelope of `1440px`, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 2rem`. Features side-by-side comparative studios (clinical scan vs. couture render).
- **Rhythm & Hierarchy:**
  - Dense grouping (`space-xs`, `space-sm`) binds telemetry labels to metric values.
  - Component cavities (`space-md`, `space-lg`) isolate distinct analysis pods.
  - Macro structural margins (`space-xl`) preserve generous breathing room, avoiding visual compression and reinforcing an unhurried, luxury salon experience.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is realized through glass refraction, luminous ambient bleed, and layered dark-matter surfaces rather than standard drop shadows:

- **Surface Tier 0 (Canvas):** `#0B0F17`. Deepest, absorbing dark slate.
- **Surface Tier 1 (Docked/Inset Modules):** `#121826` with a `1px` stroke of `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Tier 2 (Floating Glassmorphic Cards):** `rgba(18, 24, 38, 0.72)` with a `16px` backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), bounded by a hairline top highlight `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.12)` and lower boundary `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Tier 3 (Modals & Biometric Sheets):** `rgba(24, 32, 50, 0.85)` with `24px` backdrop blur, accompanied by an ambient, diffused glow: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(11, 15, 23, 0.8)`, paired with a secondary directional cast tinted with primary indigo `0 0 32px -8px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.18)`.
- **Active Scanning & Focus Elevation:** Active states produce an inner photonic luminescence (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 12px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`), simulating illuminated lens glass.

## Shapes
The structural geometry utilizes balanced, modern rounding (`roundedness: 2` / 0.5rem base radius) to communicate surgical exactness tempered by ergonomic luxury:

- **Base Components (Inputs, Segmented Controls, Badges):** `8px` (`0.5rem`). Clean, defined corners that retain technical precision.
- **Content Cards & Scan Panels (`rounded-lg`):** `16px` (`1rem`). Softened glass frames that hold live camera viewports and product recommendations comfortably.
- **Overlays, Drawers & Diagnostic Sheets (`rounded-xl`):** `24px` (`1.5rem`). Dramatic, sheltering curves that cradle user biometric summaries.
- **Floating Controls & Action Pills:** Full circular radii (`9999px`) reserved exclusively for floating capture triggers, circular biometric gauges, and interactive filter pills.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Studio Button:** Full saturation gradient from `#6366F1` to `#8B5CF6`, white bold typography (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), subtle outer ambient glow (`0 0 20px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.35)`), with an active scale compression of `0.98`.
- **Secondary Atelier Button:** Semi-translucent obsidian base (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`) with a `1px` border of `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.15)`. Text in `#F8FAFC`. Shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)` on hover.
- **Luxury Accent Button:** Subtle rose-gold metallic gradient (`#FB7185` to `#F59E0B`), used strictly for high-conversion styling unlocks and VIP checkout actions.

### Badges & Diagnostic Chips
- **Clinical Vital Chip:** Ultra-compact (`label-sm`), featuring a glowing tertiary dot (`#10B981`) next to tabular numbers. Background: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`, border: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)`.
- **Couture Palette Chip:** Swatch capsule featuring a circular dual-tone fabric/cosmetic preview with high-contrast text (`#F8FAFC`) on a `rgba(18, 24, 38, 0.8)` background.

### Cards & Diagnostic Panels
- **Biometric Scan Panel:** Frosted glass backing with active hairline borders. The upper quadrant houses real-time confidence scores and wavelength metadata; the body holds live diagnostic feeds or high-resolution editorial portrait renders.
- **Couture Recommendation Tile:** Asymmetric vertical aspect ratio (3:4) reminiscent of editorial lookbooks. Hover reveals an ambient rose-indigo underglow and frosted information tray sliding from the bottom.

### Segmented Controls & Sliders
- **Tactile Mode Switcher:** Recessed slate background (`#0B0F17`) containing an elevated glass pill indicator that slides horizontally behind the active text segment, accentuated with an inner shadow and top light catch.

### Form Inputs & Search Fields
- **Precision Inputs:** Minimal flat fields with subtle bottom line or enclosed glass containers (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`). On focus, the hairline border shifts from slate (`rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.1)`) to vibrant violet (`#8B5CF6`) with a gentle outer diffuse glow. Labels sit in all-caps micro-type above the input.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- **Selection Nodes:** Non-standard circular targets featuring a concentric ring structure. Selected state fills with an indigo core enveloped by a radiant halo ring.
